# Eurostat symmetric IOT accounting contract

## Status and purpose

This document is the economics-facing source of truth for validating the
Eurostat symmetric input-output tables used by this repository. It describes
the accounting objects and identities that must hold before disaggregation,
after disaggregation, and after reaggregation. It does not prescribe a software
container and does not replace pymrio's analytical IO system.

The immediate scope is a Eurostat product-by-product symmetric IOT for one
country, year, unit, price basis, and stock-flow role. The contract is designed
to remain general across countries, years, classifications, and split profiles.
Country, sector, product, final-demand, and supplementary-row codes belong in
source dictionaries or role mappings rather than numerical validation code.

The contract distinguishes:

1. primitive published observations;
2. independently rounded published controls;
3. exact identities generated by this repository;
4. analytical derivatives such as coefficients and Leontief inverses; and
5. structurally non-applicable cells.

Published rounded controls and generated identities must never share the same
tolerance policy.

## Standards basis

The accounting interpretation must be consistent with:

- European System of Accounts 2010, Chapter 9, supply and use tables and the
  input-output framework;
- Eurostat, *Manual of Supply, Use and Input-Output Tables*;
- Eurostat national supply, use and input-output table metadata; and
- United Nations, *Handbook on Supply, Use and Input-Output Tables with
  Extensions and Applications*.

The standards establish two fundamental balancing requirements:

1. supply equals use for every product at a common valuation; and
2. output equals intermediate consumption plus value added for every producing
   industry or product column at compatible valuations.

Repository validators must translate these principles into the exact source
layout and must not infer valuation treatment from a display label alone.

## Economic objects

### Transaction blocks

For a product-by-product symmetric table:

- `Z[i,j]` is use of product `i` as an intermediate input in production of
  product `j`;
- `Y[i,f]` is use of product `i` by final-use category `f`.

Rows of `Z` and `Y` share the product domain. The columns of `Z` use the same
product domain in a symmetric product-by-product IOT. Final-use columns are a
separate domain.

### Primary-input and value-added accounts

Rows below the intermediate-use block may include:

- compensation of employees;
- other taxes less subsidies on production;
- consumption of fixed capital;
- net operating surplus;
- net mixed income;
- gross operating surplus and mixed income;
- gross value added; and
- output.

These rows are economically distinct from product taxes, product subsidies,
trade margins, and transport margins. Their exact component set must come from
the source role dictionary.

### Supply controls

Relevant product-level controls may include:

- domestic output;
- imports of goods and services;
- total supply at basic prices;
- trade margins;
- transport margins;
- taxes on products;
- subsidies on products;
- non-deductible VAT; and
- total supply at purchasers' prices.

A product supply control is not interchangeable with a use-by-purchaser control,
even when both are stored as supplementary rows in a rectangular workbook.

### Use controls

Relevant controls may include:

- total intermediate use by product;
- total final use by product;
- total use by product;
- intermediate consumption by producing column;
- imported intermediate use by producing column; and
- imported final use by final-use category.

### Valuation matrices

Valuation information can apply across the complete use block `[Z | Y]` and may
therefore require matrices rather than a single row. Relevant matrices can
include:

- trade margins;
- transport margins;
- taxes on products;
- subsidies on products;
- VAT adjustments; and
- CIF/FOB or territorial adjustments.

The validator must compare objects only at compatible valuations. Any identity
that includes a valuation adjustment must declare the included components.

### Stock-flow roles

The repository uses three related flow roles:

- `DOM`: uses of domestically produced products;
- `IMP`: uses of imported products;
- `TOTAL`: total uses, normally the sum of domestic and imported uses.

For the same expression

\[
r_i = \sum_j Z_{ij} + \sum_{f \in F} Y_{if},
\]

its economic meaning depends on the flow role:

- for `DOM`, `r_i` is domestic output allocated to uses;
- for `IMP`, `r_i` is imported-product use and may be signed when signed stock/disposal uses dominate;
- for `TOTAL`, `r_i` is total transaction use. A separate published supply control may exist on the same product domain.

The generic expression must therefore not be named `output` without a flow-role
qualification.

## Validation classes

A transformed table is not validated by one undifferentiated notion of
"balance".  Two classes are mandatory and must be reported separately.

### Class I — accounting identities and source conservation

This class asks whether the emitted table is a valid IOT representation at the
declared boundary and whether authoritative source information was conserved.  It
contains structural/domain checks; final-demand partitions; row-use and column
controls; production-account and value-added identities; DOM/IMP/TOTAL
decomposition; declared supply and imported-use controls; V/M serialization
identities; applicability masks; and disaggregation/reaggregation conservation.
Generated identities use strict floating-point `atol + rtol * scale` comparisons
**per cell**.  They are not bitwise-equality tests and must not use one global
maximum table value to set every cell's tolerance.  Published controls continue to
use the separate source-rounding policy.

### Class II — transformation coherence

This class asks whether the disaggregation operator used donor evidence in the
economic role declared by the method.  These are not national-account identities.
For producer-column EXIO conditioning they include preservation of the declared
target child-output margin, row-child/buyer-parent mass, source parent Z cells,
zero-target-output support, invariance to donor producer-scale changes that leave
technical coefficients unchanged, and the exact-compatible fixed point.  A table
can pass every Class-I identity while failing Class II; for example a synthetic
child can satisfy `P1 = P2_ADJ + B1G` only by receiving a large negative residual
`B1G` after a scale-contaminated donor allocation.

Negative `B1G` is therefore not made a generic failure condition: signed residual
income/value-added components can be economically valid.  The transformation must
instead prove that donor technology and donor production scale were not conflated.

Valuation matrices (sometimes represented as a separate `N`-type block in other
layouts) remain policy-gated.  Their row, column and national controls are Class-I
identities only when the source role dictionary establishes the matrix, signs and
valuation bridge.  The current `ParsedEurostatIOT` exposes `Z`, `Y`, `V` and `M`;
it does not invent a universal `N` block from incomplete metadata.

## Structural invariants

Structural checks precede numerical identities.

### Labels and domains

Validators must require:

- unique row and column labels in every block;
- an explicitly declared product domain;
- an explicitly declared final-use domain;
- `Y` rows aligned exactly to the product rows of `Z`;
- complete authoritative supplementary coverage over its declared domain;
- no duplicate classification codes introduced by normalization or mapping; and
- matching country, year, unit, price basis, and flow-role metadata for compared
  objects.

Rectangular supply-use tables and industry-by-industry symmetric IOTs require
separate declared domains. A square product-by-product assumption must not be
silently applied to them.

### Applicability and missingness

The following states are distinct:

- finite non-zero observation;
- applicable economic zero;
- missing or unavailable observation; and
- structurally non-applicable cell.

A validator must reject:

- non-finite values in required applicable cells;
- silent conversion of missing observations to zero;
- finite values in cells declared non-applicable; and
- incomplete authoritative vectors such as imports by product.

### Units and valuation

For SDMX-sparse source tables, an absent observation is not automatically an
economic zero. After the actual IOT structural rows and columns have been identified,
every expected core `Z` and `Y` cell must be explicitly present and finite; otherwise
parsing fails. Structural non-cells outside that identified core are not part of this
completeness test.

Objects may be added or compared only when their units and valuation bases are
compatible. At minimum the role inventory must record:

- monetary unit and scale;
- current-price or volume status;
- basic-price or purchasers'-price basis;
- CIF or FOB treatment for imports where relevant; and
- any territorial adjustments.

## Final-demand basis

The computational final-demand basis must be explicit and non-overlapping.
Published aggregate controls may coexist with details but cannot both enter an
additive calculation.

For current Eurostat conventions, the candidate families are:

\[
P3 = P3\_S13 + P3\_S14 + P3\_S15,
\]

and either the flat investment hierarchy

\[
P5 = P51G + P52 + P53,
\]

or the nested hierarchy

\[
P5 = P51G + P5M.
\]

When `P52` and `P53` are published, `P5M = P52 + P53` is a derived display
aggregate. When those details are absent but `P5M` is published, `P5M` is the
atomic inventories-plus-valuables control and the missing detail must not be
invented. If no investment detail is published, `P5` itself is the atomic control.

Exports may be represented by `P6` or by an explicitly declared exhaustive
partition. Export details must not be assumed exhaustive from a shared prefix.

Generated geographic export residuals require an additional source/generated
distinction. When `P6` is present, it is the atomic export total. Published
`P6_B0` and `P6_U2` are retained as independently rounded geographic display
controls, while generated tables rebuild

\[
P6_D0 = P6 - P6_B0,
\qquad
P6_U3 = P6_B0 - P6_U2.
\]

The generated residual display values may be slightly signed when independently
rounded source controls cross. They are not atomic final demand and must not be
clamped or used to alter `P6`. Published partition discrepancies are checked with
the source-rounding envelope; the generated residual identities are exact.

A sparse SDMX source may omit an individual `P6_D0` or `P6_U3` display cell even
when both operands needed for the corresponding identity are explicit. In that
narrow case the parser may reconstruct only the missing residual cell, must record
its row/column/formula provenance, and must not treat the reconstructed value as an
independently published rounded control. Missing operands, missing atomic/control
cells such as `P6`, `P6_B0`, or `P6_U2`, and all other missing core `Z`/`Y` cells
remain hard source-evidence failures.

For Eurostat's consolidated `EU27_2020` SUIOT, the accounting boundary is the EU as
a whole: intra-EU trade is domestic and `P6` is export to economies outside the EU.
Consequently `P6_B0`, `P6_U2`, and `P6_U3` are structural-zero/non-applicable export
controls at that boundary. The parser may materialise those declared SDMX controls
as exact zero when their observations are globally or locally sparse, but must fail
if any explicit non-zero observation contradicts the consolidated-EU boundary.
`P6_D0` remains a published display control and, after generated-table construction,
is exactly `P6 - P6_B0 = P6`. This is a geography-specific accounting identity, not
a general licence to convert sparse SDMX observations to zero.

The additive final-use set `F` must contain exactly one representation of each
included family. Controls such as total final use and total use are never part
of `F`.

## Within-flow row identities

For every product `i` and selected non-overlapping final-use set `F`:

### Total intermediate use

\[
TIU_i = \sum_j Z_{ij}.
\]

### Total final use

\[
TFU_i = \sum_{f \in F} Y_{if}.
\]

When aggregate controls are represented as `P3`, `P5`, and `P6`:

\[
TFU_i = P3_i + P5_i + P6_i.
\]

### Total use

\[
TU_i = TIU_i + TFU_i.
\]

### Domestic-output allocation

For `DOM`:

\[
q_i = \sum_j Z^{DOM}_{ij} + \sum_{f \in F} Y^{DOM}_{if}.
\]

### Imported-product use

For `IMP`:

\[
u^{IMP}_i = \sum_j Z^{IMP}_{ij} + \sum_{f \in F} Y^{IMP}_{if}.
\]

This is a use-table row total, not the separate imports-CIF supply control `P7`.
Because the selected final-use basis contains signed stock/disposal components,
imported-product use may be signed.

### Total-use allocation

For `TOTAL`:

\[
u_i = \sum_j Z^{TOTAL}_{ij} + \sum_{f \in F} Y^{TOTAL}_{if}.
\]

## Within-flow column identities

For every producing column `j`:

### Intermediate inputs before declared valuation adjustments

\[
IC^{raw}_j = \sum_i Z_{ij}.
\]

### Intermediate consumption at the declared valuation

\[
IC_j = IC^{raw}_j + \sum_{a \in A_{IC}} adjustment_{a,j},
\]

where `A_IC` is an explicit policy-defined set. It must not be inferred from a
row label. In particular, whether taxes less subsidies on products enter a
reported intermediate-consumption control is an unresolved source-layout
question until confirmed from the transmitted table metadata.

### Output and gross value added

At compatible valuations:

\[
P1_j = IC_j + B1G_j.
\]

Equivalently:

\[
B1G_j = P1_j - IC_j.
\]

### Gross value-added components

When all required source components are present, the role dictionary must define
an exhaustive component set `R_B1G` such that:

\[
B1G_j = \sum_{r \in R_{B1G}} W_{rj}.
\]

No fixed component list may be assumed across all Eurostat presentations. In
particular, the production account may expose the gross-surplus basis

\[
B1G_j = D1_j + D29X39_j + B2A3G_j,
\]

without publishing the finer bridge

\[
B2A3G_j = B2A3N_j + P51C_j.
\]

`P51C` and `B2A3N` are therefore conditional detail, not dataset-wide mandatory
rows. When the finer rows are present, both identities are checked; when only
`B2A3G` is present, the gross-surplus identity is sufficient.

Supplementary `M` cells may be `NaN` when the source layout marks a row/column
combination missing or structurally non-applicable. Numerical M identities are
checked only on finite jointly applicable cells. The repository-generated
`M[TOTAL] = sum(V)` serialization control is enforced on generated tables, but it
is not promoted to a published-source identity.

### Row-column output bridge

For a symmetric domestic product-by-product IOT, the domestic output allocated
across product rows must equal the output reported under the corresponding
producing columns:

\[
q_i^{row} = P1_i^{DOM,column}.
\]

The generated validator must test this bridge directly on DOM.  Because the TOTAL
production account carries the same domestic-output control, it must also test

\[
P1_i^{TOTAL,column}=q_i^{row},
\qquad
P1_i^{TOTAL,column}=P1_i^{DOM,column}.
\]

The second equality belongs to the declared shared DOM/TOTAL production account,
not to blanket `V_TOTAL = V_DOM + V_IMP` additivity.  This bridge is not valid
without matching product domains and compatible valuation.

## Supply and use identities

### Total supply at basic prices

The combined dissemination has flow-specific stock-flow presentations, so the
same accounting-item code must not be given one equation solely because its row
label is present. For every product the TOTAL supply-side controls satisfy

\[
TS^{BP, TOTAL}_i = P1^{TOTAL}_i + P7^{TOTAL}_i,
\]

where `P7` is imports CIF by product. `P7` is not reconstructed from the IMP
transaction-row-use vector.

When the DOM presentation exposes `TS_BP`, it is a domestic-output control:

\[
TS^{BP, DOM}_i = P1^{DOM}_i.
\]

A separately published DOM `P7` remains source evidence but is not added to DOM
output. The IMP imported-use presentation has no domestic production/supply
`TS_BP` closure.

### Supply/use reconciliation

Supply equals use only after the compared objects have been established at a
common valuation and transformation boundary. The current `naio_10_cp1700`
implementation therefore preserves `TS_BP/P7` supply controls and transaction-use
controls independently and reports their bridge as not tested unless source
evidence authorizes it. A scalar comparison cannot substitute for that evidence.

### Purchasers'-price bridge

Where all required valuation components are available:

\[
TS^{PP}_i = TS^{BP}_i
+ TM_i + TRM_i + TAX_i - SUB_i + VAT_i + OTHER_i.
\]

The signs and component set must be declared by the valuation policy.

### Valuation-control conservation

A valuation matrix must reconcile to its applicable published controls across:

- product rows;
- intermediate and final-use columns; and
- the national grand total.

A margin matrix may have additional zero-sum constraints depending on the source
presentation. Such constraints must be declared, not assumed generically.

## Cross-flow identities

Cross-flow checks compare `TOTAL`, `DOM`, and `IMP` tables with identical domains,
units, and valuations.

### Intermediate-use decomposition

\[
Z^{TOTAL}_{ij} = Z^{DOM}_{ij} + Z^{IMP}_{ij}.
\]

This is a cellwise identity.

### Final-use decomposition

For every selected atomic final-use category:

\[
Y^{TOTAL}_{if} = Y^{DOM}_{if} + Y^{IMP}_{if}.
\]

Aggregate controls and their details must not be tested as if they were
independent additive cells.

### Imports supply and imported-use axes

`P7` is imports CIF by product on the supply side. The IMP transaction table is a
use table. They share a product classification but are not therefore the same
vector. Under the current `naio_10_cp1700` evidence the repository does **not**
assert

\[
P7_i = \sum_j Z^{IMP}_{ij} + \sum_{f \in F}Y^{IMP}_{if}.
\]

A future bridge requires explicit source/valuation evidence rather than matching
labels alone.

### Imported intermediate-use controls

For every using product or industry column:

\[
UI^{Z}_j = \sum_i Z^{IMP}_{ij}.
\]

### Imported final-use controls

For every final-use category:

\[
UI^{Y}_f = \sum_i Y^{IMP}_{if}.
\]

Together these form one imported-use control across `[Z | Y]`, even if the file
layout stores the two pieces in separate supplementary blocks.

### Domestic-output bridge

The output control associated with the total table must equal domestic output
allocated in the domestic table:

\[
P1_i^{TOTAL} = q_i^{DOM}.
\]

### Imports bridge status

The current source contract keeps `P7` supply and IMP transaction use as separate
observed/transformed objects. Neither a productwise nor a national-scalar equality
between them is promoted to a generated identity without a verified valuation and
transformation bridge.

The imported-use controls that *are* generated from the IMP table are instead

\[
IMP^V_j = \sum_i Z^{IMP}_{ij},
\qquad
IMP^M_f = \sum_i Y^{IMP}_{if}.
\]

These are column-oriented use controls and must not be confused with `P7_i`.

## Economy-wide identities

These checks are secondary to detailed product and column identities.

### Production approach to GDP

\[
GDP_P = \sum_j B1G_j + TAX^{products} - SUB^{products}.
\]

### Expenditure approach to GDP

Subject to the source treatment of territorial adjustments:

\[
GDP_E = P3 + P5 + P6 - P7.
\]

### Income approach to GDP

Where the complete income components are available:

\[
GDP_I = compensation + taxes\ on\ production\ and\ imports
- subsidies + operating\ surplus + mixed\ income.
\]

### Three-way consistency

\[
GDP_P = GDP_E = GDP_I.
\]

The exact code set and territorial adjustments must come from the source role
inventory.

## Disaggregation conservation

Accounting coherence and source conservation are separate questions. A split
may satisfy all identities while failing to preserve an authoritative source
cell, or reaggregate exactly to a source that was already internally
inconsistent.

### Intermediate transactions

For each source parent cell:

\[
Z^{before}_{ij}
= \sum_{a \in children(i)}\sum_{b \in children(j)}Z^{after}_{ab}.
\]


### Producer-column technology conditioning

Parent-cell conservation is necessary but does not by itself couple synthetic
producer columns to target-country child output. Let \(Z^{0,f}_{ab}\) be the
preliminary donor split, \(C_j\) the children of buyer parent \(j\), and
\(r^f_{a,j}=\sum_{b\in C_j}Z^{0,f}_{ab}\). Canonical child output remains the DOM
row-use margin \(x_b\); EXIO output is not a second target.

For persisted EXIO producer-output share \(s_b^{x,E}\), reweight buyer children by

\[
\omega^f_{ab}=Z^{0,f}_{ab}\frac{x_b}{s_b^{x,E}},
\qquad
Z^{*,f}_{ab}=r^f_{a,j}\frac{\omega^f_{ab}}
{\sum_{c\in C_j}\omega^f_{ac}}.
\]

Because \(Z^{0,f}_{ab}\propto D^{E,f}_{ab}\) inside a donor-backed parent cell and
\(s_b^{x,E}\propto x_b^E\), the prior is proportional to
\(D^{E,f}_{ab}x_b/x_b^E=A^{E,f}_{ab}x_b\): EXIO technology at Austrian child
output. Row-child/buyer-parent masses, emitted row totals, DOM output, and source
parent cells are therefore preserved. Use the same DOM production scale for DOM
and IMP producer columns.

This closed-form conditioning is not RAS/GRAS or optimisation. Positive Austrian
output with zero EXIO output support, or positive row mass supported only on
zero-output producer children, is an evidence conflict and fails explicitly.

### Final use

For every atomic final-use category:

\[
Y^{before}_{if}
= \sum_{a \in children(i)}Y^{after}_{af}.
\]

Signed investment atoms require an additional child-level constraint only for
`DOM`: each emitted child's complete domestic row use is its domestic-output margin
and cannot be negative. The split may therefore redistribute the selected atomic
investment component within its source parent while conserving that parent's value
exactly. `IMP` child rows are imported-product use and may be signed; they are not
rebalanced to mimic a non-negative supply vector. Use the most granular available
atomic investment control (`P52`, nested `P5M`, or aggregate-only `P5`), and never
invent omitted `P52`/`P53` detail merely to perform DOM balancing.

### Primary inputs and supplementary observations

Every primitive applicable row must conserve its source cell over split columns.
Generated identity rows must instead be recomputed from the split transaction
blocks and then validated.

### Valuation matrices

Every primitive valuation cell must conserve its source value over emitted child
rows and columns.

### Applicability

A transformation must not convert non-applicability or missingness into an
economic zero.

### Balancing-scope semantics

For global balancing, an output-share vector may be a hard child-output target.
For local balancing, the clean expanded transaction row totals are authoritative
and the output shares are expansion priors. Reports must state which contract was
used.

## Class-I coverage ledger

For generated `naio_10_cp1700` tables, the blocking accounting layer must cover,
where the source policy authorizes the required evidence:

- structural domains, uniqueness, finiteness and applicability;
- the one non-overlapping atomic final-demand basis and all declared P3/P5/P6
  partitions;
- `TFU`, `TU`, `CPA_TOTAL` and the complete DOM row-output bridge;
- `P2_ADJ`, `P1 = P2_ADJ + B1G`, the available gross/net value-added component
  identities, and `D1 >= D11`;
- cellwise `Z_TOTAL = Z_DOM + Z_IMP` and atomic
  `Y_TOTAL = Y_DOM + Y_IMP`;
- imported intermediate/final-use controls on their declared V/M axes;
- declared P7 geography and `TS_BP = P1 + P7` supply controls;
- generated named `M[TOTAL] = sum(V)` serialization controls;
- exact conservation of primitive Z/Y/V/valuation objects through
  disaggregation/reaggregation; and
- persisted `x` and `A = Z diag(x)^{-1}` sidecars, with shared domestic `x` and
  direct `x = DOM row use` validation at both split and reaggregated resolutions.

Candidate equations whose valuation or source role is unresolved remain explicit
`not_tested` results.  In particular, the current evidence does not license
`P7 = IMP transaction-row use`, `TS_BP = TU`, a complete purchasers'-price
valuation bridge, or three-way GDP equality.  Listing an equation is not authority
to enforce it.

## Reaggregation validation

Reaggregation must recover every authoritative pre-split object:

- intermediate transactions;
- atomic final use;
- primitive primary-input rows;
- primitive supplementary rows;
- valuation matrices;
- applicability masks;
- units and role metadata.

Generated controls must be recomputed after reaggregation. Validation reports
must distinguish:

- source conservation failures;
- accounting-identity failures; and
- published-control discrepancies.

## Published versus generated tolerance

### Published controls

Independently rounded source controls may differ from sums of independently
rounded components. Their tolerance must use the existing quantisation-aware
source-control diagnostic, including the reporting quantum and number of terms.

### Generated identities

Rows and controls generated by this repository must satisfy their equations
within strict floating-point absolute and relative tolerances. They must not
receive a source-rounding allowance.

### Reaggregation

Reaggregation compares generated values derived from the same monetary source
and should therefore use strict numerical tolerance.

## Pymrio boundary

Pymrio remains the analytical IO system. The repository validator is a source
and transformation accounting layer, not a competing Leontief implementation.

Only validated computational objects should enter pymrio:

- additive intermediate transactions;
- one non-overlapping final-demand basis;
- the correct domestic output vector;
- applicable primary-input and environmental extensions; and
- direct final-demand extensions where present.

Before conversion, unresolved missing or non-applicable values must not enter
computational matrices. After `calc_system()`, pymrio output and coefficients
must be compared with the validated repository calculations.

## Validation stages and blocking policy

The same invariant library should be invoked at these boundaries:

1. parsed published source;
2. immediately before disaggregation;
3. immediately after disaggregation;
4. after reaggregation;
5. before pymrio construction; and
6. after pymrio system calculation.

Initially, new validation runs in shadow mode on real source data. Once source
peculiarities are documented:

- structural defects are blocking;
- generated accounting failures are blocking;
- cross-flow decomposition failures are blocking;
- reaggregation failures are blocking;
- published rounded-control diagnostics follow declared source policy; and
- unavailable optional identities are reported as not tested, not silently
  passed.

## Generic `naio_10_cp1700` full-table policy

`EurostatIOTAccountingPolicy` is the dataset/table-type policy for the
product-by-product `naio_10_cp1700` layout. It is not a country, year, product
count, or product-order profile. The Austria 2021 observation is kept as a
separate evidence record, so a future country can satisfy the same policy with
its own product domain and reviewed source schema.

The policy selects exactly this atomic computational final-demand basis:

\[
\{P3\_S13, P3\_S14, P3\_S15, P51G, P52, P53, P6\}.
\]

It validates the nested published controls `P3`, `P5M`, `P5`, `P6_B0`, and
`P6` separately. `TFU`, `TU`, `TOTAL`, and regional P6 controls are never
added to the atomic basis. This preserves a complete non-overlapping use block
while still auditing every displayed source control.

The full report covers structural domains, final-demand partitions, `CPA_TOTAL`
in both V and M, the declared `P2_ADJ`/`D21X31` bridge, production-account
identities, DOM/IMP/TOTAL Z and atomic-Y decomposition, imported-use controls,
`P7` geography, `TS_BP`, and explicitly named V-to-M grand totals. It does not
assert blanket `V_TOTAL = V_DOM + V_IMP` or `M_TOTAL = M_DOM + M_IMP`: these
blocks contain flow-specific account controls rather than one uniformly
additive matrix.

Published-source checks call the existing quantisation-aware source-rounding
diagnostic. Generated-table mode uses strict floating-point tolerance and does
not receive a source-rounding allowance. Missing mandatory schema entries are
structural failures; unavailable optional evidence is reported as
`not_tested`; and a source table parsed after sparse SDMX zero filling reports
applicability as `not_tested` rather than claiming that zero proves
applicability.

ESA establishes GDP production, expenditure, and income formulas, but this
parsed IOT does not itself establish the required national product-tax,
purchasers-price, CIF/FOB, and territorial-adjustment controls. GDP checks
therefore remain explicit `not_tested` results. Automatic disaggregation,
reaggregation, and pymrio integration remain later validation stages.

## Unresolved questions that must precede implementation

The source-role inventory must resolve or explicitly defer:

1. the exact meaning and valuation of `P2_ADJ` in each Eurostat table used;
2. whether and where product taxes, subsidies, margins, and VAT are supplied as
   rows, matrices, or separate datasets;
3. the exhaustive value-added component set for the selected presentation;
4. the exhaustive export partition, if export details are used;
5. the exact location and meaning of imported-use controls across `V` and `M`;
6. the applicability pattern of identity rows under intermediate and final-use
   columns;
7. CIF/FOB and territorial adjustments required for GDP checks; and
8. whether current source files contain all three flow roles at identical
   classifications and valuations.

No numerical validator should silently choose an answer to these questions.
